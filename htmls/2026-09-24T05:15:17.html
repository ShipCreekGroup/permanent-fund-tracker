Fund Performance vs. Benchmarks

Strategic Benchmarks

APFC’s benchmarks are designed to reflect the portfolio’s structure and global market realities, guiding performance while keeping the Fund resilient through all market cycles. To assess how effectively the Fund is managed and whether it is meeting its long-term investment goals, APFC’s Board of Trustees has approved three distinct benchmarks, each serving a unique role in measuring performance, discipline, and strategic value.

APFC’s monthly performance report provides a consistent update on the Fund’s performance relative to the established benchmarks.

Passive Index: Added Value
This short-term performance indicator is based on a blend of passive indices reflective of a traditional stocks/bonds portfolio. This benchmark evaluates whether the added sophistication of APFC’s diversified strategy delivers long-term value.

Performance Benchmark: Peer Comparison 
The performance benchmark offers a peer comparison. This indicator combines indices across all asset classes, reflecting the Fund’s target asset allocation. It is designed to reflect the daily decisions made by APFC’s investment managers and to serve as a measure of whether APFC’s execution and market calls have added value over specific time periods.

Return Objective: Long-Term Target
This benchmark, set by the Board, is an annualized return of inflation (Consumer Price Index or “CPI”) + 5%. It measures whether the Fund is meeting its long-term real return goal, preserving and growing value after inflation. It also guides asset allocation and aligns investment strategy with the Fund’s long-term obligations and protects intergenerational value.

Transparency in Reporting

Financial & Performance Reports

APFC’s financial statements and performance reports offer detailed insight into the Alaska Permanent Fund’s financial position, investment management, and long-term performance. Materials include monthly financial statements, historical data and projections, performance reporting by asset class, and benchmark comparisons used to evaluate results over time. Together, these reports support transparency, accountability, and informed review of the Fund’s management.
